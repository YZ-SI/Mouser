# #255 [Bug] DPI preset and custom button settings don't load after restart. Deleting last_device.json fixes it until next restart

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**State:** `ready-for-agent` (cause is a guess; the behavior contract is clear)

## Root cause (Mouser evidence)

- `core/hid_gesture.py:2858-2870` `_try_connect` loads `last_device.json` (`:101-123`) and reorders HID candidates and device indexes by it. The cache is written after a successful divert at `:3284-3297`; TTL is 30 days (`:77`).
- Replay of DPI/SmartShift is gated on a hid-ready transition (`core/engine.py:1338`) plus a one-shot startup fallback after 3 s (`core/engine.py:1620-1625`), which is blocked once a replay was requested this launch (`core/engine.py:1278`). If the first connect after boot picks a different or half-ready interface/slot, diverts and DPI are not applied and nothing repeats.
- Symptom match: middle click (Windows hook, no HID) works, while Mode Shift and gesture (need HID diverts, `core/hid_gesture.py:3077` onward) and DPI (HID write) do not. That points at a HID session that is open but not successfully diverted.
- The attached `mouser.log` and `last_device.json` were not available to me, so why the cache changes the outcome is not located.

Confidence: guess on the cache mechanism; likely that boot-time connect completes without a verified divert/replay.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** After a cold boot, diverted buttons and saved DPI must be applied reliably, without relying on any persisted device cache.

**Current behavior:**
After reboot with an MX Master 3S over Bolt, saved DPI is not sent to the mouse and diverted buttons (Mode Shift, gesture) do not run their remapped actions until `last_device.json` is deleted. Hook-only buttons (middle click) work.

**Desired behavior:**
Every successful HID++ session setup, including the first after boot, ends in a verified state: all needed diverts acknowledged by the device, and saved DPI/SmartShift written and read back. If verification fails, the session is torn down and retried with bounded backoff. No on-disk cache may change which interface or device index is used in a way that can skip this verification (a cache, if kept at all, is a hint and falls back to a full scan on any miss).

**Key interfaces:**
- HID session `open_and_prepare()`: returns success only after diverts are verified.
- Settings replay API, invoked on every session-ready event, not once per process launch.
- Config shape unchanged (`config.json` `settings.dpi`, `profiles.*.mappings`).

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Unit test with a fake HID device that ignores the first N requests after open: the session retries and ends with diverts verified and DPI written.
- [ ] Unit test: session-ready fired twice in one process produces a replay each time.
- [ ] Unit test: a corrupt or stale cache file never prevents a successful connect.
- [ ] Manual, MX Master 2S over Unifying: set DPI 1600 and remap a diverted button; reboot; without opening the GUI, the remap works and DPI reads 1600 within 15 s of login, on 5 consecutive reboots.

**Out of scope:**
- Bolt-specific quirks that cannot be tested on a 2S
- The `last_device.json` file format
- Resume from sleep (#263) and mouse power cycle (#249), which share the replay entry point
